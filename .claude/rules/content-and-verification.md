# Rule: Content & Verification

## Evidence-based manufacturing claims

American-made claims **require supporting evidence**. Classify at the product
level where possible using the enum in `docs/verification-methodology.md`. Store
enum values; display human-readable labels.

**Never infer** domestic manufacturing from any of these alone:

- U.S. headquarters
- American founder / U.S. workers
- American imagery or a name containing "USA"/"America"
- "Designed in America"
- The brand having _some_ U.S.-made products

Do not present a mixed-sourcing brand as if all products are domestically made.

## Evidence records

Every claim links evidence with: source URL, source title, evidence note,
evidence type, date accessed, confidence score, review status, reviewer, review
date, last-verified date, dispute status, internal notes. Evidence cannot reach
`approved` without a source. Internal notes and unreviewed evidence are never
public.

MadeHere is **not** a certification body — never describe it as one.

## Paid content firewall

A paid subscription must never alter a classification, guarantee verification,
remove accurate unfavorable info, suppress a legitimate correction, buy an
editorial recommendation, or change the evidence standard. Classification and
evidence approval are admin-only and independent of billing.

## Disclosure

Sponsored and affiliate content is conspicuously labeled every time — as
components, not incidental copy. Paid placements are never marked as independent
editorial.

## Automation limits

- No scraping in violation of terms/anti-bot controls; no verbatim copying of
  product descriptions or copyrighted articles.
- No fabricated sources, reviews, discounts, or brand data.
- Never auto-certify "Made in USA" or auto-publish conflicting claims.
- AI output is not evidence. Store the underlying source separately from any
  AI-generated summary. Search-result snippets are never sole evidence for
  important claims.

## Seed / demo data

Fictional only, clearly labeled, removable via script. Never invent manufacturing
claims about real companies. Admin UI warns that demo data must not be published
as factual content.
