# Revenue Model — MadeHere

## Primary: affiliate commissions

Tracked outbound links via `/go/[slug]`. Supports direct brand affiliate
programs, affiliate networks, retailer programs, custom referral agreements, and
non-affiliate direct purchase links. **At launch we track outbound clicks only** —
we do not fabricate conversion, sales, commission, or partnership data.

## Primary: paid brand profiles (annual)

| Plan | Placeholder price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic** | Free | Listing, limited info, limited products, website link, profile-claim access, correction access. |
| **Verified** | $999/yr (placeholder) | Expanded profile, more products, evidence-review workflow, profile analytics, promotions/discount codes, annual review, verified designation **only when evidence supports it**. |
| **Featured** | $3,999/yr (placeholder) | Everything in Verified + featured category placement, eligible homepage placement, newsletter/social promotion opportunities, product-launch promotion, enhanced analytics. |

Prices are placeholders pending approval before production payments.

### Paid-content firewall (non-negotiable)

A subscription must never alter a manufacturing classification, guarantee
verification, remove accurate unfavorable information, suppress a legitimate
correction, guarantee an editorial recommendation, or change the evidence
standard. "Verified" tier ≠ "Verified Made in USA" classification — the tier is
a subscription level; the classification is an evidence outcome. The UI must not
conflate them.

## Secondary (future-enabled, disclosed every time)

Sponsored shopping guides, sponsored founder/factory stories, featured product
placements, newsletter sponsorships, exclusive discount campaigns, display
advertising, seasonal gift-guide sponsorships. All conspicuously labeled;
never marked as independent editorial.

## Future (architecture-ready, not in MVP)

Consumer premium memberships, direct marketplace commissions, price alerts,
saved products/wish lists, data/trend reports, white-label product data.

## Disclosure requirements

Affiliate disclosure renders near every purchase button. Sponsored content
carries a conspicuous sponsored label as a component. See
`docs/affiliate-operations.md`.
